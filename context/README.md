# context/

This folder holds what your AIOS knows about **you** — the stable facts that make Sage
useful instead of generic. `/onboard` writes most of it from your `aios-intake.md` answers;
you edit it any time.

| File | What it holds |
|------|---------------|
| `about-me.md` | Who you are, your role, your background, how you work. |
| `about-work.md` | What you do and where the value of your work lands. |
| `priorities.md` | What you're focused on right now (this season / next 90 days). |

Keep these current. When your priorities shift, tell Sage — or edit the file directly.

Two related files live **outside** this folder:
- Your **voice** (how Sage should *sound*) → `references/voice.md`.
- Your **connections** (the tools Sage can reach) → `connections.md` at the repo root.
