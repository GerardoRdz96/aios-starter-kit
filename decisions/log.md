# Decisions Log

Append-only record of decisions and **why** you made them. Future-you (and Sage) will thank
you. When you settle something that matters, add an entry. When Sage helps you decide, it'll
suggest logging it here.

**Format:** newest on top.

```
## [YYYY-MM-DD] <short title>
**Decision:** what you decided.
**Why:** the reasoning, the alternatives you passed on.
**Revisit if:** what would make you reconsider.
```

---

## [2026-06-04] Adopt an AIOS
**Decision:** Stand up a personal AI Operating System from the aios-starter-kit template.
**Why:** Wanted my AI to remember context across sessions and compound knowledge instead of
starting cold every chat.
**Revisit if:** the upkeep ever outweighs the leverage.
